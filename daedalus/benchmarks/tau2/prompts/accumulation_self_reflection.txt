## What to look for
Work out the root cause of this outcome — not a summary of what happened. Consider several dimensions:
- Tool usage: a wrong tool or arguments, calls in the wrong order, or acting on the wrong record.
- Policy compliance: a required check skipped, an action taken without the mandatory explicit confirmation, or something the policy forbids.
- Information gathering: acting before authenticating the user or before fetching the records the action depends on.
- Communication: computing or retrieving the right information but never stating it to the user.
- Ending: stopping, transferring, or letting the conversation end before every part of the request was done.

## What to produce
1. Analysis: one to three sentences naming the root cause of the success or failure. Do not narrate the trajectory.
2. Lessons: one to three durable lessons for future tasks. Write each as a single line beginning with `- `:
   - When <situation the agent will meet again>, <do this>[, because <the reason>].
   Keep every lesson generalizable — no ids, names, order numbers, or values from this task — and concrete enough to act on (not "be careful"). Each lesson should address a distinct failure mode or winning move, phrased in the situation wording that would actually appear next time.

**One partition you must include: what the task delivers.** Some requests here are satisfied by reporting a value the solver worked out from the data; others by changing state. These are genuinely exclusive, a designer can tell them apart without judgement, and a run that produces only one kind has covered half the ground — so give each its own tag, say in both definitions that they exclude each other, and set the two shares to sum to about 1. Decide the split from what a person would realistically be asking this environment for, and do not set the answer-reporting share near zero: it is the kind most easily forgotten and it is not a lesser task.

## Facts about this environment
- Read the actual data in several apps before deciding what to design; the good tasks come from what is genuinely there, not from method names.
- Some records only make sense against another app's (a shared expense and the payment that settles it, a message and the file it refers to). Reconciling two apps is one legitimate source of work — but a single app explored in depth is another, equally good one.
